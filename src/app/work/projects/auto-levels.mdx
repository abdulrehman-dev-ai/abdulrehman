## Client and business context

Auto Levels is an automotive customization business based in Lahore, Pakistan. Its public website presents premium vehicle modifications, services, and customer contact channels.

## My role

I worked with Auto Levels on its IT and web presence, including the business website and related technical operations. My role combined website delivery with practical technology management rather than an internship exercise.

## Work delivered

- Built and maintained the business-facing web presence for automotive services and customer enquiries.
- Structured service information to help visitors understand interior, exterior, and performance upgrades.
- Supported the operational IT environment and coordination of technical requirements.
- Worked on responsive presentation suitable for visitors browsing from mobile and desktop.

## Outcome

The business website is publicly accessible at [autolevels.pk](https://www.autolevels.pk/). This case study reflects real client work; it does not claim unmeasured traffic, sales, or conversion improvements.
