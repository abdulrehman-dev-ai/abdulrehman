## The business challenge

An international yacht-registration provider needed more than a brochure website: customers and approved dealers required structured applications, accurate quotations, private document submission, and ongoing case visibility.

## My contribution

Led the implementation and delivery of the registration platform, including the frontend experience, backend workflows, integrations, environment separation, quality checks and client handover.

## What shipped

- **Two registration journeys:** public customer submissions and an authenticated dealer portal with dealer-attributed applications.
- **Distinct services:** Polish flag registration and related services, plus a Delaware Deletion Certificate workflow.
- **Authoritative pricing:** server-side calculations and saved pricing snapshots; Delaware deletion uses a fixed $99 USD fee.
- **Case management:** administrative dashboard, status transitions, history, dealer management and customer tracking.
- **Private documents:** protected uploads for registration evidence and application-specific supporting materials.
- **Operational communications:** transactional customer and dealer email workflows with delivery safeguards.
- **Release engineering:** Cloudflare Workers, D1, Supabase Auth and Storage, Resend, GitHub, production/staging separation, regression testing and deployment smoke checks.

## Delivery

**Live production platform.** The application was delivered and subsequently enhanced with additional dealer and Delaware functionality. The public site is available at [oceanyachtregistration.com](https://oceanyachtregistration.com/).

## Engineering decisions

- Customer and dealer data is linked through explicitly scoped records.
- Sensitive configuration and uploaded identity documents remain outside the public portfolio.
- Staging and production use distinct backend resources to reduce deployment risk.
- Pricing is validated by the server instead of trusting totals submitted by the browser.
