## Overview

Built and delivered a vessel-registration website with public customer registration, dealer onboarding and a partner portal.

## Engineering scope

- Customer and dealer-specific registration reference number formats.
- Dealer onboarding, approval and role-aware administrative workflows.
- Transactional emails for registration events and dealer updates.
- Cloudflare Workers, D1 database migrations and GitHub-based source control.
- Responsive interface improvements, deployment verification and operational handover.

## Outcome

Deployed to production and handed over in October 2026. Customer data, private configuration and credentials are intentionally excluded from this public case study.
