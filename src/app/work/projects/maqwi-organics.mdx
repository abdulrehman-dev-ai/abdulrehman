## Business objective

Build a premium digital storefront for a Pakistan-based organic products brand, presenting honey, desi ghee, achaar and itar with a coherent identity and a mobile-friendly shopping experience.

## Implementation scope

- Developed the storefront theme and responsive brand presentation on WordPress with WooCommerce.
- Organized approved product details, imagery, prices and quantities in a source-controlled configuration.
- Planned variation and inventory handling, including shared stock for different achaar pack sizes.
- Defined Pakistan-only flat-rate shipping at PKR 300 per order.
- Built a guarded source deployment process with file-integrity checks and backup preservation.
- Documented security, recoverability and checkout test requirements before launch.

## Technology

WordPress, WooCommerce, PHP, theme customization, GitHub, FTPS-based hosting, backups and deployment safeguards.

## Current status

**Pre-launch / still being validated.** The temporary storefront can be viewed at [maqwiorganics.gt.tc](https://maqwiorganics.gt.tc/).
