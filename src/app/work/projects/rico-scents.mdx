## Project brief

Rico Scents is a fragrance e-commerce project built on Shopify. The client requested a premium, polished shopping experience, using an existing scents retailer as a design reference while establishing a distinct brand identity.

## Work in progress

- Developing a responsive storefront with a luxury-oriented visual direction.
- Organizing products and categories for a usable catalogue.
- Configuring US-market product pricing and initial inventory.
- Iterating on premium product presentation and storefront usability.
- Reviewing product publication, stock visibility, and site readiness.

## Delivery status

**Active client project.** Store configuration and published product checks are part of the implementation process. Full launch-readiness, checkout, payment processing and business outcomes have not been independently verified here.

## What this demonstrates

Hands-on Shopify catalogue operations, e-commerce UI/UX, branded merchandising and practical storefront implementation.
